# Contributing to unbork-playbooks

Thanks for the interest. This pack is AGPL-3.0-or-later, like unbork.

## Licensing & copyright

You keep the copyright in your contributions. Pull requests are
accepted under the same contributor licence agreement (CLA) as
[unbork](https://github.com/open-unbork/unbork), and one signature covers
both repositories:

- On your first PR the `CLAAssistant` check fails and a bot comments.
  Read the [individual agreement](https://github.com/open-unbork/unbork/blob/dev/legal/cla/v1/ICLA.md),
  then post the sentence the bot quotes. The check turns green.
- The email your commits carry must be linked to your GitHub account:
  on a PR from a fork, the `LinkedCommits` check fails otherwise.
- The agreement is a licence, not an assignment, and the maintainer
  promises that your contribution stays available under
  AGPL-3.0-or-later (or another OSI-approved licence).
- A company that owns its employees' work signs the
  [corporate agreement](https://github.com/open-unbork/unbork/blob/dev/legal/cla/v1/CCLA.md)
  instead.

See [`legal/cla/`](https://github.com/open-unbork/unbork/blob/dev/legal/cla/README.md)
in unbork for both texts, how to sign, and what is recorded when you do.
